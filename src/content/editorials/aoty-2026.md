---
title: "Why this project won"
---

Between thoughtful production choices, the pockets Sideshow finds almost efffortlessly on each track, and surprisingly introspective lyrics, this album sort of embodies exactly what I wanted out of this project: to get put onto a new artist I'd otherwise have overlooked.

My opinions on some of the albums have shifted over the years. Some grew on me, while others kind of simmered after the initial hype. But even with *Tigray Funk* being a relatively early listen this year, each replay has only reinforced my case for keeping it at the top spot for the year.
