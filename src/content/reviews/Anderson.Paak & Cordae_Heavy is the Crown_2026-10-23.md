On paper, this has a combo that should be an absolute layup: Paak, Cordae, and 9th Wonder on production. And it's definitely a casual bop. But it isn't the near-guaranteed hit one would think from this trio. Not to say that it isn't worth the listen. And even now, I find myself appreciating some of the tracks like *Airbender* a bit more. And the sample of Bill Wither's *Lovely Day* is cheeky on the similarly named track *A Good Day* . So maybe it's a matter of giving it a few more listens, but it isn't the best performance either rapper has ever given. Luckily for them though, their lows still make for an enjoyable listen.
