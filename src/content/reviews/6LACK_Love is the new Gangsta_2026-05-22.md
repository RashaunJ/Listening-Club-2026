I'm torn on this one. Obviously I want to judge him on the lyrical content/vocal performances primarily. But I didn't find myself nodding to this one as much as I thought. It's odd, because although you can't entirely discount production, the producers really didn't give him a lot to work with on this album and he himself didn't do enough to carry that difference. Maybe a revisit but this might not get a lot of replay value from me.
