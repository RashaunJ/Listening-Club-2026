A bit harder to parse through as this is two tracks, each around 18 minutes. Shh/Peaceful is definitely a bit more upbeat and pleasant to listen to in the 2nd half. In a Silent Way starts off with a slow melancholic drone before a harsh shift into a more up-tempo midsection. Ending is a high point. I really *wanted* to appreciate this in the moment, but I don't know if it captured me in the way I was anticipating. Still there's not doubt of the artistry on display.
