This feels like a classic, complete album. Going into this pretty much only hearing their songs on occasion via radio play and Guitar Hero, this album kind of makes it clear why they've endured for so long. Obviously you have the classic title track Master of Puppets on here but honestly it didn't end up being my favorite on that album. Welcome Home felt a bit introspective and Disposable Heroes was a bit more politically aware then I was expecting from them. To that point I wouldn't consider it overtly conscious as a project since that's not what I think they were going for but still surprising.

As much as I'll still label this dad rock, I enjoyed it a lot.
