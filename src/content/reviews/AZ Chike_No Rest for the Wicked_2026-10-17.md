Certifiably Bumps in the Whip (TM). First got put onto AZ Chike from a few of his features, but this is the first project I'm listening to from him. And I have to say I'm impressed. It's very easy to play off an album like this as carried by production, but I feel like he's absolutely bouncing on the tracks. The kind of pockets he finds himself have you scrunching your face and nodding. Production really leans into that west coast vibey wave that I first heard in command in Kendrick's GNX, and you're getting variations of that same sound here. Obligatory mention to Look Like My Mama which features Tyler the Creator and has crazy replay value.

Just had a lot of fun listening to this one.
