This one had me confused initially. I had to relisten to the first half and my first thought was 'I'm not seeing the appeal' And to a certain point I'm still holding onto that thought. 

But that later half.

The 2nd half is for me personally such a tonal shift that it feels like an entirely different album. Shirking lyrics, it's primarily instrumental, and when his voices does come in it's treated as another instrument in the composition. I really vibed with Warszawa and Subterraneans is such a masterful ending to the album. Spacey and intimate, it's arguably one of if not the highlight. It'd be higher if not for the first half.
