Drake albums can be good when you don't have a bitch in your ear telling you it's shit. Jokes aside, it's alright. 1 of a triple release after the internet spent the better part of 2 years virtually flogging him. If you think about it too hard it's got a healthy serving of cornball lyrics. Clearly he's still not over the beef considering he's throwing sneak disses over 2 year laters. Plus apparently he's salty at A$AP Rocky? One thing that'll always be at least solid on a Drake album is the production. And I don't think anyone can deny he can ride a beat. Looking at it just as a product that you don't analyze too deeply, it's an alright dare I say catchy listen. But man, any deeper and it's a whine fest at points. Which I guess is par for the course, and if you're a Drake stan you'll love exactly that. And I have Drake projects I like, but this one is "bump it in the shop and just sing the lyrics" material. Whelming.
