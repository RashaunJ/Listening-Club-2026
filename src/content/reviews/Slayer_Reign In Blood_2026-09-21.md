Comes in furious and doesn't really let off the throttle the entire time. Not too familiar with the thrash genre but I imagine this being the prototypical kind of album for it. With the runtime being as short as it is, it really feels like you're just getting a short shot of adrenaline and it leave as quickly as it enters. I found some of the lyrics cheesy at times but it kind of suits the vibe.
