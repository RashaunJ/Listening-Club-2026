Coming into this, I acknowledge my biases. I love house music in any form, and I'm a sucker for good vocals layered on top of said house music. And this has it in spades. I'd first came across the track Ladida in a playlist which prompted me to try the full album.

Long story short: this entire album is a banger. She's wearing her influences on her sleeve here, directly channeling elements of 90's RNB, house and dance music here. And while it's very easy for it to feel like an artist is wearing a genre like a costume, she nailed the vibe perfectly. Everything from the production, the subject matter involving at times vague themes of heartbreak and loss would fit right in a setlist for a DJ playing out of an abandoned warehouse. Even the fashion on the music video for *Doing it Too* is a heavy nod to the time period.

No skips on this, just listen to it.
