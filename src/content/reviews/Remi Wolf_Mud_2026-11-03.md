So this is one of those examples of an album that I think grows on you, at least to an extent. The first listen: it was on a verge of a skip as a whole. Remi going for more of a country twist really wasn't just resonating with me. I say this as someone who has only really periodically listened to a few of her tracks. But giving it an honest second shot...there's some stuff to like her. I don't think it's changed my perception entirely to an album I'd regularly have on my rotation, but there's some good tracks to parse through here. The title track in particular feels like it was made to be *the* single for this album, but in all honesty I think I vibed with tracks like Champagne and Jamie more. In particular, I really enjoyed the more ballad-like ending track Yellow Rose which felt like a pretty grand send off to the whole project.

It's a nice little pop/country fusion that I think I'd probably enjoy a bit more if I was already a decently big fan of hers.
