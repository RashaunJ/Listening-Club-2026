I feel this one warrants more of a longer blurb. Currently on the third listen of this and I think the overarching theme for this would be spiritual. A bit contrived but it really does feel like the kind of sound that takes you out of yourself directly into the experience. I was unfamiliar with Desert Rock, but the vibey instrumentation, and psych rock adjacent sounds made for a rich soundscape. The plucky guitar, claps, and soothing vocals make for something that is immediately alluring and recognizable as masterful.
