I'm starting this off with an apology to Toro y Moi. I didn't realize you were moving like that. My main exposure with him before was primarily through 2019's *Outer Peace*, and the occasional single here and there, so I thought I had general bead on their style. Apparently I was wrong, because I definitely got thrown off when I discovered this was an ambient album. And a pretty solid one at that. Best listened to in one sitting, the soundscape on this feels minimal but intentional. Songs like Lull feel like you're sinking into the earth, the sound of birds accompanying the dreamy piano chords while tracks like Poor Connection throw you deep into space with synths that feel reminiscent of the 70s era of early space obsessed thematic in synth and rock. Packaged together, this ends up being an EP that feels beautiful from front to back.

I wouldn't try to pick a single out of this project. I think it's best listened to in one sitting.
