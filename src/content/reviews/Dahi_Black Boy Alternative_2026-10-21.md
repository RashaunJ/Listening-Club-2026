A surprisingly feature packed album featuring appearances from Kendrick Lamar, Vince Staples, Childish Gambino, Channel Tres and more. Truth be told I never knew about Dahi until this album came across my way but he has a pretty big resume, producing for Kendrick, Drake, Big Sean, J Cole and others. For me this feels like a pretty solid producer album with a cohesive, peaceful theme. The first half especially so just feels like a warm summer evening. Although I didn't come away *loving* the album, I certainly feel like it has at least a few tracks worth having a permanent spot in a playlist.
